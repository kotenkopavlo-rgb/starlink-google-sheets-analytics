# Task 04 — Revenue by Subscription Plan

## Business Question

Which subscription plan generates the most revenue?

## Analysis

Total revenue was calculated for each subscription plan using revenue after discount.

## Key Findings

The Residential plan generates the highest revenue, with more than twice the revenue of the second-highest plan. The Residential plan also has the largest customer base, with more than three times as many customers as the second most popular plan.

## Data Quality

One customer in the RawData table does not have a matching subscription record and was excluded from the subscription-level revenue analysis.

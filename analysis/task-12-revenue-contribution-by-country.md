# Task 12 — Revenue Contribution by Country

## Business Question

Which countries contribute the most to total revenue?

## Analysis

Total revenue and revenue share were calculated for each country. The results were visualized using a horizontal bar chart.

## Key Findings

Spain generates the highest total revenue, while the United Kingdom generates the lowest. Overall, the difference in revenue share between countries is relatively small, at approximately 0.8 percentage points.

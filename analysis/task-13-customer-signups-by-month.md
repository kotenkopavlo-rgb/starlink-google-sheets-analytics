# Task 13 — Customer Sign-ups by Month

## Business Question

How many new customers signed up each month?

## Analysis

Customer sign-ups were grouped by month using the sign-up date. The monthly customer counts were visualized using a line chart to identify changes and fluctuations over time.

## Key Findings

The number of new customers ranged from 95 to 159 per month. Overall, monthly customer sign-ups remained relatively stable, mostly falling within the range of 100 to 150 customers.

# Task 06 — Customer Value by Subscription Plan

## Business Question

Does the plan with the largest customer base also generate the highest revenue per customer?

## Analysis

Customer count, total revenue, and average revenue per customer were calculated for each subscription plan.

## Key Findings

The Residential plan generates the highest total revenue, while the Business plan has the highest average revenue per customer. This indicates that the Residential plan is the main revenue driver due to its large customer base, while Business customers generate more revenue individually.

# Task 15 — Revenue Share vs Customer Share by Country

## Business Question

Do countries generate a share of revenue proportional to their share of customers?

## Analysis

The analysis compares each country's share of the total customer base with its share of total revenue.

For each country, the following metrics were calculated:

- Customer Share
- Total Revenue
- Revenue Share
- Difference between Revenue Share and Customer Share

The difference was calculated in percentage points:

`Revenue Share - Customer Share`

A positive value means that a country's share of revenue is higher than its share of customers. A negative value means that its share of revenue is lower than its share of customers.

A bar chart was used to visualize the difference between the two shares.

## Key Finding

The report shows that revenue share is closely aligned with customer share across countries. Overall, the differences between the two metrics are small, ranging from -0.26 to +0.18 percentage points.

## Data Quality Note

Revenue calculations use Revenue After Discount from the RawData table. One customer has no matching subscription record and is excluded from subscription-level revenue calculations.

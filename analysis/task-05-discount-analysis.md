# Task 05 — Discount Analysis

## Business Question

How do discounts affect monthly revenue?

## Analysis

Customers were grouped into three discount categories: No Discount, Small Discount, and Large Discount. Total revenue and average revenue per customer were calculated for each category.

## Key Findings

A large discount does not guarantee higher average revenue per customer. Customers without a discount generate the highest average revenue per customer.

## Limitation

Retention and churn data are not available, so the long-term impact of discounts on customer value cannot be determined.

## Business Recommendation

Consider testing smaller discounts and analyzing customer retention, churn, and upgrade rates before making decisions about discount strategies.

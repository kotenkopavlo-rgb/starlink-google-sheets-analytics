# Task 07 — Discount Effectiveness

## Business Question

How effective are different discount levels in generating revenue per customer?

## Analysis

Customers were grouped into three discount categories: No Discount, Small Discount, and Large Discount. Average revenue per customer and revenue share were calculated for each category.

## Key Findings

A large discount does not guarantee higher average revenue per customer. Customers without a discount generate the highest average revenue per customer.

## Limitation

Retention and churn data are not available, so the long-term impact of discounts on customer value cannot be determined.

## Business Recommendation

Consider testing smaller discounts and analyzing customer retention, churn, and upgrade rates before making decisions about discount strategies.

## Business Hypothesis

A 50% introductory discount on the Mini plan could attract new customers and encourage them to upgrade to higher-tier plans after the introductory period. This hypothesis would require retention and upgrade data to validate.

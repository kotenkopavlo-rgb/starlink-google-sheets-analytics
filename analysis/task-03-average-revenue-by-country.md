# Task 03 — Average Revenue per Customer by Country

## Business Question

Which countries have the highest average revenue per customer?

## Analysis

Average revenue per customer was calculated for each country using revenue after discount.

## Key Findings

Japan and Spain have the highest average revenue per customer, while Germany has the lowest. Overall, average revenue per customer is relatively consistent across countries, with only a small difference between the highest and lowest values.

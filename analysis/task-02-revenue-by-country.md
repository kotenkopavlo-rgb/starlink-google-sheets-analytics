# Task 02 — Revenue by Country

## Business Question

How much revenue does each country generate?

## Analysis

Total revenue was calculated for each country based on revenue after discount.

## Key Findings

Spain generates the highest total revenue, while the United States generates the lowest. The difference is relatively small, indicating a fairly even revenue distribution across countries.

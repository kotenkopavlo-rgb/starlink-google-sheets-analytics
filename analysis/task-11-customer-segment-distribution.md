# Task 11 — Customer Segment Distribution by Country

## Business Question

How does customer value distribution differ across countries?

## Analysis

Customers were grouped into Low Value, Medium Value, and High Value segments for each country. A 100% stacked column chart was used to compare the customer value structure across countries.

## Key Findings

The customer value structure is relatively consistent across countries. Low and Medium Value customers make up the majority of the customer base in every country, while High Value customers account for a smaller share.

Japan has the highest share of High Value customers, while Germany has the lowest.

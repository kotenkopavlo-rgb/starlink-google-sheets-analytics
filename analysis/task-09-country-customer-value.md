# Task 09 — Customer Value by Country

## Business Question

Which countries have the most valuable customers?

## Analysis

Customer count, total revenue, average revenue per customer, High Value customer count, and High Value customer share were calculated for each country.

## Key Findings

Spain generates the highest total revenue, while Japan has the highest average revenue per customer and the highest share of High Value customers. This indicates that Japan has a particularly valuable customer base, even though Spain generates more total revenue.

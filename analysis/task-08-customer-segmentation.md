# Task 08 — Customer Segmentation

## Business Question

How is our customer base distributed across value segments?

## Analysis

Customers were segmented based on Revenue After Discount:

- Low Value — below $100
- Medium Value — $100 to below $200
- High Value — $200 and above

Customer count, total revenue, and average revenue per customer were calculated for each segment.

## Key Findings

High Value customers account for approximately 10% of the customer base but generate approximately 21.5% of total revenue. This indicates that High Value customers are disproportionately important to the business.

Customer value does not always correspond directly to the subscription plan, as discounts can move customers into a different value segment.
